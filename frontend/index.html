How to Use Median

1
Paste a URL

Copy any YouTube video or playlist, SoundCloud track or set, or Bandcamp album URL and paste it into the input field above.

2
Click Validate

Median will check the URL, detect the platform, and fetch the track or album info — title, artist, duration, and artwork.

3
Choose your format

Pick Audio Only (MP3 / FLAC / AAC), Video Only (MP4 / MKV / WebM), or Cover + Audio for SoundCloud/Bandcamp tracks with artwork embedded as video. In Cover + Audio you can upload your own cover image and choose its aspect ratio, resolution, and output format.

4
Pick the songs you want

For an album or playlist, every track is listed with a tick box — untick any song you don't want and it won't be downloaded. Select all / Deselect all is there for quick changes, and the counter above the list shows how many are picked. Leaving everything ticked downloads the whole album as usual. Picked songs keep their original album track numbers in the filename (choose tracks 2, 5 and 6 and you get 002, 005, 006), so they still sort correctly next to the rest of the album.

5
For playlists: merge or keep separate

Toggle Merge into single file to combine all tracks into one file with chapter markers — you can skip between songs inside the file. With merge on, you can also enable Crossfade to blend each track into the next and pick the overlap length. If a crossfade can't be applied (e.g. a track is too short), the merge falls back to hard cuts and a note appears under the download.

6
For albums: grab the artist's whole discography

Toggle Download every album by this artist and Median looks up the artist's releases page, then lists every album it finds with a checkbox each — untick the ones you already have, or use Select all / Deselect all. Each ticked album is queued as its own download and lands in its own Artist - Album folder, with its own cover, tags and description.md. Every other setting on the form (format, bitrate, merge, description) applies to all of them. They download a few at a time rather than all at once, so a large discography works through the queue steadily.

7
Hit Download

The download runs in the background. Track it in the Active Downloads section. When done, the file is available for 15 minutes before auto-cleanup — press Keep on the finished download to protect it. Album covers are embedded automatically (MP3/FLAC/AAC tags, MP4/MKV containers); WebM can't hold embedded art, so the cover is saved separately and a note tells you so.

8
Chapters & the description file

Turn on Include description.md before downloading to bundle a markdown file with your download: the tracklist (for merged albums, with crossfade-corrected timestamps), Bandcamp lyrics (also embedded into each file's tags so music players show them), the source link, the release date, the album tags as hashtags, and a credits line. On a finished merged album, the ≡ Chapters button shows the chapter table with Copy for YouTube description — paste it into a YouTube description and YouTube creates the chapters (it ignores chapters embedded in the file itself).

9
History, queue & themes

The History panel lists past downloads — a ⬇ button appears next to a song's name while its file is still on the server, so you can grab it again. The sun/moon button switches dark/light mode, and the palette button next to it cycles through six extra color themes — one click per theme, wrapping back to the original at the end.
